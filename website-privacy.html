Website privacy

Effective October 6, 2026. This notice covers optional Google Analytics on the public Celephais Labs, Snap! Screen Translator and SailCat websites.

Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you select Allow analytics. Declining sends no Analytics requests. Use Cookie choices at the bottom of any public page to change your choice. Your choice is stored on this browser for each website separately. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences disable Analytics.

What is measured

With your permission, Google receives public page visits, page titles, browser and device information, approximate geographic information and cookie identifiers to help us understand website traffic. Analytics cookies expire after 90 days. Website addresses sent to Analytics omit query parameters and fragments, and referrers include only the referring origin. Advertising personalization and automatic form, search, download and outbound-link tracking are disabled.

We do not add Analytics to SailCat sign-in, account, billing, administration or API pages. This website tracking does not measure VPN connections, VPN traffic, screenshot content, translations or activity inside either mobile app. Product privacy policies describe those products separately.

Google and questions

Google processes this information through Google Analytics. See Google's privacy policy. For questions, contact celephais.labs@gmail.com.
