User controls

Privacy choices

Snap! Screen Translator does not use a hosted account for normal app use, so most privacy controls are local device, in-app, or store subscription controls.

If you need help with any of these steps, contact snapappsupport@gmail.com.

Delete screenshots

Delete screenshots using the normal controls on your device. Snap does not need a separate account dashboard for screenshots that remain under your device control.

Remove offline models

Open Snap settings and choose Manage Offline Models to remove downloaded language packs you no longer want stored on your device.

Clear local app data

Because normal use does not require a hosted account, uninstalling the app removes local preferences and operational state according to platform behavior.

Manage or cancel subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple or Google account settings, not through a Snap web account.

Manage Apple Subscriptions Manage Google Play Subscriptions

Manage ad privacy

Open Snap settings for ad privacy choices and ad reporting. Free-tier ads are requested as non-personalized ads, and active subscribers remove ads while subscribed.

Report an ad

Use Settings > Report an Ad or email snapappsupport@gmail.com with what you saw and when it appeared.

Support email records

If you email support, we may retain that correspondence as needed to respond, troubleshoot, and keep a support history related to your request.

Ask a privacy question

For questions about screenshots, translated text, local storage, purchases, or support records, email snapappsupport@gmail.com.

No separate account deletion flow is required for normal use

The current Snap! Screen Translator build does not require creating a hosted Snap account. If the product later adds accounts or server-side user profiles, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated with the account deletion process before release.

Privacy questions: snapappsupport@gmail.com
