Privacy posture

Useful screenshot translation should not need an account.

Snap is built as a utility. Translation use is centered on local processing, with purchases managed by the App Store or Google Play.

Privacy Details
  • No account is required for normal use.
  • Free-tier ads use consent controls and non-personalized ad requests.
  • Screenshot images, recognized text, and translated text are not sent to the ad SDK for targeting.
  • Screenshot text and translation output are intended to be processed locally unless you choose to share them.
  • On-device translations are powered by Google Translate.
  • Support email only receives the information you decide to send.

Supporter access is managed by the App Store or Google Play.

The app offers optional weekly, monthly, and yearly auto-renewing plans. Eligible accounts may see a store-managed free trial or introductory offer before confirmation. Restore Purchases is available in the app if access does not update automatically.

Quick answers

Does Snap require an account?

No. Normal app use does not require creating a Snap account. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

Can screenshots contain private information?

Yes. Only translate screenshots you are comfortable processing on your device, and do not send private screenshots to support unless you choose to include them.

Where do I get help?

Use the support page or email snapappsupport@gmail.com with your device, platform version, app version, target language, and what happened.
